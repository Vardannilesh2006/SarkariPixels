## Why Government Application Forms Reject Photos

Every year, over 30% of competitive exam applicants (SSC CGL, UPSC Civil Services, BPSC, RRB NTPC, IBPS PO) face form rejection or admit card delays due to improper photo and signature uploads. Most application portals enforce strict automatic filters that reject files if:

1. **File Size Exceeds Portal Maximum**: SSC portals mandate 20 KB to 50 KB, while mobile cameras take 3MB+ photos.
2. **Incorrect Dimensions (Pixels or Centimeters)**: SSC requires **3.5 cm × 4.5 cm** (413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI), whereas UPSC requires **350 × 350 pixels (square format)**.
3. **Invalid File Format**: Portals only accept JPG/JPEG formats. PNG, WEBP, or HEIC files from iPhones trigger upload errors.
4. **Unclear Background**: SSC and UPSC require plain white or light-colored backgrounds. Dark or patterned backgrounds are flagged automatically.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Resize & Compress Exam Photos

To ensure your application is accepted without uploading sensitive personal documents to third-party cloud servers:

1. **Take a Clear Photo**: Stand in front of a plain white or light wall with natural daylight. Ensure both ears and eyes are visible.
2. **Crop to Passport Frame**: Crop out excess background so your face occupies 70% to 80% of the frame.
